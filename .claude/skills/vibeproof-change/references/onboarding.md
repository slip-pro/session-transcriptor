# First use in a project

Classify the project: new, existing, moving from another setup, or already using Vibeproof.

**Existing project.** Infer its purpose, audience and main user journey from its sources, keeping
what you saw apart from what you guessed. Note who controls content or data (CMS, database) and how
stage differs from production if that's documented. Don't map every page. Show the inferred purpose
concretely and ask whether it's right. Respect the existing stack, hosting and design; propose no
migration.

**New project.** Ask what it is and who it is for. When choosing a stack, explain it in terms of cost,
ownership and how easy it is to hand over, with your recommendation.

**One round of questions**, together with setup. Reuse answers already given; leave unknowns marked
unknown and defer what blocks nothing. Approving the installation doesn't confirm a guessed business
goal. Ask about branding, domain and payments when they first matter. Turn on no telemetry and add
no supplier as a side effect.

**Explain the way of working** through the first task, not by listing machinery, and don't claim
installation proves the product works. No templated welcome, no preference questionnaire: learn how
they like to communicate as you go.

**Record** the confirmed purpose, audience and open assumptions in `product/product.md`, marking
guesses as reconstructed until confirmed. Then continue the task they gave, or recommend one place to
start. If onboarding was interrupted, finish only the missing part.
